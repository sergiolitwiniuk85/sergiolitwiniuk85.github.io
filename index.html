About

Geneticist and bioinformatics analyst with 10+ years at CONICET building end-to-end NGS pipelines across genomics, transcriptomics and metagenomics. I focus on reproducible, production-grade workflows (Nextflow, Snakemake, HPC) and have contributed to projects with the European Genome-phenome Archive (Barcelona), NIAB and the Wellcome Trust Sanger Institute. I recently completed a Master's in Data Science & AI (UCM) and now work at the intersection of structured bioinformatics and agentic AI / protein language models — turning biological questions into maintainable, scalable code.
